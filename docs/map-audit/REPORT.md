## Method (short)
1. `tools/map_audit_extract.mjs` builds every place module headless with the real kit (same stubs as tests/smoke.mjs) and records, by wrapping the physics and a few K calls, every wall, walkable surface, ramp/stair/escalator, ground hole, zone, door opening and asphalt/sidewalk paint, tagged by module. Nothing in js/ is touched.
2. Footprints: a top-down 0.5 m mask per module = slabs/roofs higher than 2 m (>=20 m²) plus regions enclosed by tall (>=4 m) ground walls; underground claims (gotomall) use B1 slabs. Each claimed OSM outline is compared with the connected built mass within 8 m of it (neighbouring claims removed, interior holes filled): IoU, area ratio, centroid offset, principal-axis rotation (only when both shapes are elongated, eccentricity > 1.4), max/p95 edge deviation (saturates near 8 m by construction), OSM area left empty, built area outside the outline.
3. Underground: B1 mask vs the OSM band and 신반포로; B1-B4 station masks vs the 3/7/9호선 `rail` polylines. Exits: every OSM exit vs the nearest stair/escalator head (a ramp dropping >= 2.5 m from street level) and ground hole. Junctions: endpoints, floors, zone membership and a straight-line walk with the real collision. Roads: built asphalt strips vs the nearest OSM road, asphalt outside OSM corridors, built mass inside carriageway corridors. Pins and _meta entrances vs OSM POIs, footprints and walls.
4. Severity = error in metres x prominence weight (1.0 main complex ... 0.3 peripheral); see findings.json. Rank is a triage order, not a measurement.
5. Sanity checks (snapshot of the run that wrote this file): banwon (IoU 0.91, offset 1.44 m) and the eight banpo_shoppingtown blocks (IoU 0.978-0.989, offsets <= 0.1 m), shinsegae (IoU 0.997) and newcore come out near zero, as they should since they extrude the OSM polygon. `tools/map_audit_selfcheck.mjs` rasterises the render meshes themselves (upward faces above 2 m) and compares with the collision-derived mask: IoU banwon 0.96, lebon 0.93, banpo_shoppingtown 0.95, gyeongbu_east 0.97, newcore 0.97, with no mesh-only or collision-only blob above 15 m² except lebon eave scraps (<= 29 m²). A first version of the mask missed roofs that have no collision (K.hpoly / K.slab(walk:false) / non-solid prisms; gyeongbu_east disagreed by 486 m²) and counted vehicle tops; both were fixed by recording those primitives and dropping untagged surfaces. Because other modules also build inside some claimed outlines, empty area is attributed with the union of all modules (JW: 3397 m² empty on its own, 0 m² after shinsegae 787 m² and centralcity 389 m² are counted). `tests/shot.mjs`/`mapshot.mjs` could not be run: the Playwright browser is not installed in this environment (nothing was downloaded).
