# Modeling standard (all place modules)

Goal: someone who knows the real place should recognise it in first person — outside AND inside, every real floor.

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/PLACE_API.md`, your brief `docs/briefs/<id>.md`, your research `data/research/<id>.json`, `js/places/_junctions.js`. Inspect the real footprint(s) in `data/osm.json` (e.g. `node -e "const o=require('./data/osm.json');console.log(JSON.stringify(o.buildings.filter(b=>[ID].includes(b.id))))"`) and POIs near your site (`o.pois` filtered by x/z).
2. Floors: model EVERY real floor (above and below ground) that the research lists, with real floor-to-floor heights. Exterior height = sum of floors (+ parapet/roof structures). Each floor: `K.slab` (with holes for escalator voids / atriums / stairwells), a `K.zone` naming the floor's real use (`sub` lists representative tenants/categories), shop partitions with **real brand/tenant names on signs**, fixtures (racks, shelves, counters, display tables, tables & chairs for restaurants, flower buckets, bus benches…), column grid, ceiling (slab underside) with light panels (`'emit'`), a few people (`K.crowd`).
3. Vertical circulation where it really is: escalator pairs (`K.escalator`, up+down) through voids, stairs (`K.stairs`) in cores, and at least one `K.elevator` that lists ALL floors with names. Every floor must be reachable on foot from the entrance.
4. Exterior: real footprint, real facade look (material, colour, window rhythm, signage letters, canopies) per research/road-view descriptions; entrances (openings in `K.polyWalls`) on the real sides facing the real streets; the main sign big and legible. Rooftop equipment.
5. Underground spaces: the street ground is a plane at y=0 seen from above only — any space below y=0 needs its own ceiling (slab underside or `K.hpoly(mat, poly, y, false)`), and stairwells reaching the street need `K.groundHole(poly)` over the opening. Underground floor tops use `K.LEVELS` (B1=-6, B2=-11, B3=-16, B4=-21).
6. Junctions/wells (`js/places/_junctions.js`): honor every entry naming your module (openings, floors at pa/pb, corridors if you are the owner, holes for wells).
7. Spawns: 3–8 `K.spawn` (entrance outside, each key floor). Set `default: true` on none (engine picks).
8. Budget: target 40–100k quads (gotomall up to 150k). `node tests/smoke.mjs <id>` prints quads. Prefer `K.box`/`K.prism`; share materials; no per-frame allocation.
9. Process: build → `node tests/smoke.mjs <id>` (must print OK) → `node tests/smoke.mjs <id> --dump --quiet` (lists zones/spawns/elevators) → screenshots `node tests/shot.mjs --places <id> --city 0 --out shots/<id> --views "…"` (≥ 6 views: exterior from street eye level at the real approach, each important floor interior, vertical circulation) → LOOK at every PNG → fix what looks wrong (black/missing faces, floating objects, z-fighting, unreadable signs, walls blocking entrances, wrong scale) → repeat. Also run `node tests/smoke.mjs <id> <neighbour…>` with modules you connect to when their files exist.
10. Never edit files outside your module / research / shots. Never git commit.
11. Final answer: file path, smoke result line, quads, list of floors built, spawns, screenshot paths, known gaps, engine bugs (if any).
